## Method and limits

PDFs were fetched with `curl` and read with `pdftotext -layout` in WSL; HTML was read directly. Conformance levels, hedging, quantification and commitment language were counted with `grep` across every extracted document, then remark structure was read by hand on one criterion common to all of them (1.4.3 Contrast) so documents could be compared like with like.

**Extraction failures.** Six downloaded but would not extract, all imageless-text or scanned PDFs: JSTOR, SAGE Journals, Artstor, Duke University Press, Springshare LibGuides, DOAB. Canvas and Figma would not download. Harvard's accessibility site blocks automated fetches, so its guidance pages are not in the corpus.

**Biases worth stating.**

- **Publishing and library platforms are over-represented**, because university procurement pages are the only place a large unfiltered sample is publicly indexed. Their register is likely more formal than a small agency's.
- **Atlassian, Zoom and GitHub contribute three documents each.** Where the three agree they are counted as one voice, because they are one template.
- **Nothing here is from a small web agency.** That stratum barely publishes. GovReady, SciTech and Roberts Wesleyan are the closest available.
- **A third of the corpus carries no report date at all**, which is itself a finding rather than only a gap in this table.
